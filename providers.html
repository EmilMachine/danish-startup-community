DIY vs Lawyer

DIY (templates/Virk.dk):

  • Simple ApS registration
  • Standard employment contracts
  • Basic service agreements

Hire a lawyer:

  • Fundraising (investors require it)
  • Complex ownership structures
  • International operations/contracts
  • IP protection and licensing
  • Disputes or terminations

When to Hire an Accountant

Day 1:

  • If unfamiliar with Danish tax rules
  • Want to focus on product, not accounting
  • Foreign founders without CPR/Danish banking

Can DIY initially (with software):

  • Simple revenue (< 10 transactions/month)
  • No employees yet
  • Comfortable with Dinero/Billy + TastSelv

Definitely hire when:

  • First employee (payroll complexity)
  • Raising capital (clean books for due diligence)
  • Annual report deadline approaching
  • Revenue > 1M DKK (volume justifies cost)

Opening a Business Account

Requirements:

  • CVR number (company registration)
  • Founder CPR or passport
  • Articles of association
  • In-person visit (often required, even with online banks)

Timeline:

  • Digital banks (Lunar): 1-2 weeks
  • Traditional banks: 2-4 weeks
  • Foreign founders: 3-6 weeks (may require in-person visit to Denmark)

Tips

  • Start early: Bank account needed before company registration (for ApS capital deposit)
  • For foreign founders: Lunar and some others allow remote setup
  • Multi-currency needs: Consider Revolut/Wise as secondary accounts
  • Banking relationships matter: Traditional banks helpful for loans later
