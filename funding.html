Innovationsfonden (Innovation Fund Denmark)

Denmark's primary innovation funding body.

  • Innobooster: 200k - 5M DKK, covers up to 35% of project costs
  • Project duration: Up to 24 months
  • Eligibility: SMEs with CVR, either 100k DKK raised in last 3 years OR 250k DKK gross profit
  • Innofounder: Up to 6M DKK for research-based startups
  • InnoMission: Mission-driven innovation projects (green tech, life science, digital/critical tech)
  • Application: Rolling basis throughout the year, competitive selection
  • Requirements: Innovation potential, commercial viability

Vækstfonden (Growth Fund)

Provides loans and guarantees to startups with commercial potential.

  • Loans: 500k - 12M DKK, favorable terms
  • Loan guarantees: Up to 75% guarantee on bank loans
  • Co-investment: Can co-invest with VCs
  • Requirements: Viable business model, growth potential

Grant Tips

  • Start early: Application processes take 3-6 months
  • Focus on innovation: Highlight technical/commercial novelty
  • Match funding: Many programs require co-financing
  • Reporting: Grants come with milestone and financial reporting requirements

VC Fundraising Tips

  • Warm intros: Get introductions through founders, advisors, or events
  • Traction first: Most VCs want to see revenue or strong user growth
  • Know your metrics: CAC, LTV, churn, MRR - be ready to discuss
  • Nordic rounds: Typical seed in Denmark is €500k-1.5M
  • Lead investor: Find one lead, others will follow

Accelerator Tips

  • Apply broadly: Acceptance rates are 1-5%
  • Consider equity: Typical take is 5-10%
  • Network value: Alumni connections often more valuable than funding
  • Time commitment: Expect full-time for duration

Tax Incentives

R&D Tax Credit (Skattekreditordning)

Tax credit for research and development expenses.

  • Rate: 22% of eligible R&D costs
  • Maximum: 5.5M DKK per year
  • Eligible: ApS/A/S companies with R&D activities
  • Application: Annual, with tax return
  • More info: SKAT R&D credit

Employee Stock Options Tax

Favorable tax treatment for employee stock options (ligningslovens § 7P).

  • Tax rate: ~27% at exercise (vs ~52% income tax)
  • Requirements: 3-year vesting minimum, company < 10 years old
  • Cap: Options worth up to 10% of annual salary

Investment Deductions for Angels

Incentivize angel investments in your startup:

  • Angels can deduct losses: From capital income
  • Requirements: Shares held at least 3 years
  • Note: Not as generous as UK/US angel tax incentives
