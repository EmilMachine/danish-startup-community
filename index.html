Quick Start

New to starting a company in Denmark? Follow this sequence:

  1. Legal Setup - Register ApS (20,000 DKK minimum capital) with Virk.dk
  2. Operations - Set up SKAT, accounting, banking
  3. Funding - Explore grants and investors
  4. Hiring - Understand employment law and equity
