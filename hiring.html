Employee vs Contractor

Use contractors when:

  • Project-based work (e.g., design, one-off development)
  • Testing before hiring full-time
  • Specialized expertise needed short-term

Hire employees when:

  • Core team members
  • Long-term relationship expected
  • Offering equity compensation

Misclassification risk: SKAT can reclassify contractors as employees if they work exclusively for you, follow your schedule, use your equipment, etc. This results in back taxes and penalties.

Employment Contract Requirements

Must be provided within 1 month of start date:

  • Job title and description
  • Start date
  • Salary and payment schedule
  • Working hours
  • Vacation entitlement
  • Notice period
  • Pension scheme details

Termination Rules

Notice periods (by law):

  • First 3 months: 14 days
  • 3-6 months: 1 month
  • 6 months - 3 years: 3 months
  • 3+ years: Up to 6 months (increases with tenure)

Severance pay: Required if employee has 12-17+ years of service (1-3 months salary).

Important: Denmark has strong employee protections. Get legal advice before terminating employees to avoid wrongful dismissal claims.

Total Employer Costs

Beyond gross salary, expect these additional costs:

  • Pension: 8-12% employer contribution (typical)
  • Vacation accrual: 12.5% of salary (5 weeks)
  • AES (social contributions): ~1-2% of salary
  • ATP: Small annual fee (~1,200 DKK/year)
  • Insurance: Workers' compensation, liability
  • Total: Salary × 1.25-1.30 as true cost

Benefits Startups Typically Offer

  • Pension: Required, typically 8-12% total contribution
  • Health insurance: Optional, ~2,000-4,000 DKK/year per employee
  • Lunch: Popular perk, fully/partially subsidized
  • Phone/laptop: Standard for most roles
  • Home office allowance: ~1,000-2,000 DKK/month
  • Learning budget: 5,000-20,000 DKK/year for courses/conferences
  • Equity: See equity section below

Equity & Stock Options

Equity Types

Type Tax Treatment Best For
Stock Options (§7P) ~27% at exercise (favorable) Most employees
Restricted Stock (§7H) ~52% at grant (income tax) Rarely used
Warrant Programs Capital gains (~42% on profits) Founders, advisors
Phantom Stock ~52% at payout (income tax) Cash-equivalent equity

§7P Stock Options (Most Common)

Favorable tax scheme for employee stock options:

  • Tax rate: ~27% (AM-bidrag + share income tax) at exercise
  • Requirements:
    • Company < 10 years old at grant
    • 3-year minimum vesting
    • Value cap: 10% of annual salary (per year)
    • Exercise price = fair market value at grant
  • Advantages: Much lower tax than salary (~52% income tax)

Vesting Schedule

Standard vesting structure:

  • 4-year vesting: Most common
  • 1-year cliff: No equity vests until 1 year, then 25% vests
  • Monthly vesting: After cliff, remaining vests monthly over 3 years
  • Accelerated vesting: Sometimes triggered by acquisition

Setting Up Stock Options

  1. Create option pool: Typically 10-20% of company at founding
  2. Get 409A-equivalent valuation: Fair market value for exercise price
  3. Board approval: Document option grants in board minutes
  4. Option agreements: Signed contracts with each recipient
  5. Register with SKAT: File for §7P treatment

Recommend hiring a lawyer for option plan setup. See Service Providers.

Foreign Hires & Visas

EU/EEA Citizens

  • No work permit needed - Freedom of movement
  • Registration: Apply for CPR number upon arrival
  • Timeline: Can start work immediately

Non-EU/EEA Citizens

Require work and residence permit before starting.

Work Permit Schemes

Scheme Requirements Processing Time
Pay Limit Scheme Salary ≥ 552,000 DKK/year (2026, updated annually Jan 1) 1-2 months
Supplementary Pay Limit Salary ≥ 446,000 DKK/year (2026, updated annually Jan 1) 1-2 months
Fast-track Scheme Employer certified, various salary thresholds 2-4 weeks
Positive List Job on official shortage list 1-2 months
Establishment Card Non-EU entrepreneurs (own startup) 2-4 months

Fast-Track Scheme (Recommended)

Get your company certified for faster processing:

  • Requirements: Financially sound, comply with tax/labor laws
  • Benefits: 2-4 week processing, bring family immediately
  • Cost: Certification fee + per-applicant fees
  • Apply: New to Denmark - Fast-track

Pay Limit Scheme

  • Salary threshold: 552,000 DKK/year for 2026 (adjusted annually on Jan 1). Verify current threshold at New to Denmark.
  • Supplementary threshold: 446,000 DKK/year for certain positions
  • No certification needed
  • Processing: 1-2 months
  • Best for: Senior hires above threshold

Tips for Hiring Foreign Talent

  • Start early: Visa processing takes 1-4 months
  • Salary matters: Pay limit scheme (552k DKK) easiest if you can meet threshold
  • Supplementary option: 446k DKK threshold available for certain roles
  • Get fast-track certified: Worth it if hiring multiple non-EU employees
  • Relocation support: Help with CPR, bank account, housing
  • Use immigration lawyers: For complex cases or appeals
