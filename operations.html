Tax & SKAT

Corporate Tax Rate

  • Rate: 22% on company profits (verify current rate at SKAT)
  • Payment: Paid in two installments (March and November)
  • Returns: Annual tax return due 6 months after fiscal year end

SKAT Registration

SKAT is Denmark's tax authority. Registration is automatic when you register your company with Virk.dk.

  • SE number: Central tax number (automatic)
  • TastSelv Erhverv: Online portal for tax filings - tastselv.skat.dk
  • Digital post: All official tax correspondence is digital via e-Boks

Tax-Deductible Expenses

Common startup expenses that are tax-deductible:

  • Office rent and utilities
  • Software subscriptions and cloud services
  • Salaries and contractor fees
  • Professional services (legal, accounting)
  • Marketing and advertising
  • Business travel (with documentation)
  • Office equipment and hardware

Accounting

Accounting Requirements

  • Bookkeeping system: Required by law (digital or physical)
  • Document retention: 5 years for all financial documents
  • Annual report: Required within 5 months of fiscal year end
  • Audit requirement: Only if 2 of 3 criteria met:
    • Revenue > 9M DKK
    • Assets > 4.5M DKK
    • Employees > 12 full-time

VAT (Moms)

VAT Basics

  • Standard rate: 25% on most goods and services
  • Registration threshold: 50,000 DKK annual turnover
  • Voluntary registration: Can register below threshold
  • Filing frequency: Quarterly (or monthly if turnover > 50M DKK/year)

When to Register

Situation VAT Registration Required?
Selling physical products in DK Yes (if > 50k DKK/year)
Selling SaaS to DK consumers (B2C) Yes (from first kroner)
Selling SaaS to EU businesses (B2B) No (reverse charge)
Selling SaaS outside EU No
Consulting services Yes (if > 50k DKK/year)

VAT Returns

  • Portal: TastSelv Erhverv on SKAT
  • Deadlines: 1 month and 10 days after quarter end
  • Input VAT: Deduct VAT paid on business expenses
  • Output VAT: VAT collected from customers

EU VAT Rules

  • B2B sales: Customer pays VAT in their country (provide their VAT number)
  • B2C sales: Complex rules, may need to register in customer countries
  • OSS scheme: One-Stop-Shop for simplified EU B2C VAT reporting

Payroll & A-skat

A-skat (Withholding Tax)

Employers must withhold income tax from employee salaries.

  • A-skat rate: Based on employee's tax card (skattekort)
  • Payment: Monthly to SKAT
  • Reporting: Annual eIndkomst report

Labor Market Contributions

Contribution Rate Paid By
AM-bidrag (labor market tax) 8% Deducted from salary
ATP (pension) ~1,200 DKK/year Split employer/employee
AES (employer social security) Varies Employer

Payroll Services

  • Salary.dk: Popular for small companies
  • Zenegy: Modern, startup-friendly
  • Lønservice Danmark: Traditional option
  • Your accountant: Many offer payroll as add-on

Annual Compliance

Annual Deadlines

Requirement Deadline Submitted To
Annual report (årsrapport) 5 months after fiscal year end Erhvervsstyrelsen (via Virk.dk)
Tax return (selvangivelse) 6 months after fiscal year end SKAT
eIndkomst (payroll report) Monthly + annual reconciliation SKAT
CVR data update Within 2 weeks of changes CVR register (via Virk.dk)

Annual Report Requirements

All ApS companies (and legacy IVS companies) must file an annual report including:

  • Balance sheet
  • Income statement
  • Management commentary
  • Notes to financial statements
  • Signatures from management and board

Penalties for Non-Compliance

  • Late annual report: Fines from 1,500 DKK, potential forced dissolution
  • Late tax return: Interest charges + penalties
  • Late VAT filing: Fines from 800 DKK per late return

Important: Set up calendar reminders for all deadlines. Many startups hire an accountant to manage annual compliance.
