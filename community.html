Tips for Getting Involved

For New Founders

  • Start with TechBBQ: Best overview of DK ecosystem
  • Join online communities first: Easier than in-person when starting
  • Attend 1-2 meetups/month: Consistency beats one-off attendance
  • Be helpful: Share knowledge before asking for help
  • Coffee chats: Request 1-on-1s with founders ahead of you

For International Founders

  • English widely spoken: Most events have English options
  • Online communities: TechBBQ and other communities welcome non-Danish speakers
  • International meetups: Look for expat/international tech events
  • Relocation support: Many organizations help newcomers

Making the Most of Events

  • Set goals: "Meet 3 founders" not "network generally"
  • Follow up quickly: LinkedIn connection + message within 24h
  • Give before you ask: Offer help, make intros
  • Quality over quantity: 3 deep conversations > 20 business cards
